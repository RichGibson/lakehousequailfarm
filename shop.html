How Ordering Works

1. Reach Out

Email or message us with what you'd like and when you'd like it. For eggs, cartons are limited to what the flock lays, so we'll confirm availability and reserve your order.

2. Pickup or Delivery

Collect your order at a pre-arranged pickup spot in Clearlake Oaks or at our next farmers market day in Upper Lake. Free delivery in Clearlake Oaks and Clearlake. Delivery to Lower Lake, Upper Lake, and other areas can be arranged with a $20 minimum purchase and/or regular orders for stores and restaurants.

3. Enjoy Farm-Fresh

You are purchasing legally compliant, washed, and sanitized eggs. All eggs are good for two weeks from purchase date. Please keep them refrigerated and use them before the expiration date marked on the carton.
