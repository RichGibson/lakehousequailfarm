🐦 Small Farm · Big Flavor

Farm-fresh quail eggs from our Lake County micro-farm.

We raise happy, well-fed coturnix quail for eggs. These tiny eggs aren't just cute — they are packed with flavor and nutrition. We are California Certified Egg Handlers and Lake County certified Producers for farmers markets. We strive to provide a safe, ethically-sustainable, quality product by raising healthy, happy birds and carefully following all mandated cleaning and handling regulations.

Shop Quail Eggs & Goods Meet the Flock

Wholesome and sustainable, from our family to yours.

Our quail are treated humanely and only take up a tiny footprint. Yet they produce a lovely bounty of colorful, delicious eggs.

Farm-Fresh Quail Eggs

Our hens are spoiled pets, not livestock. We gather their eggs daily, dote over them, and thank them for their contribution. Their colorful eggs have a rich, creamy yolk perfect for ramen, pickling, or baking.

Handmade Keepsakes

We make quail-themed items, one-of-kind keepsakes, and can even do custom engraving — all out of our family's home workspace.

Rooted in Lake County

We are a family micro-farm on the shores of Clear Lake. The goodness in every egg we sell represents the care we put into our flock. No big warehouses, just us and the quail.

From Our Kitchen

One egg makes all the difference in a bowl of ramen.

A soft-jammy quail egg, halved over a steaming bowl of shoyu ramen, is the easiest way to fall in love with these little eggs. They're rich, small enough for two bites, and just as good soft-boiled, pickled, or fried sunny-side up on toast.
